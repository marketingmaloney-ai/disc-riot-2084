# DISC RIOT 2084: art brief

Every art subagent works from this brief. The creative director is the main Claude session, and it approves or rejects every asset.

## Quality bar
Each asset must match or beat the original *Windjammers* (1994) Neo Geo art: readable silhouettes, strong color identity per character, clean outlines, and lively poses. The target look is smooth, high-resolution anime in the *Windjammers 2* / KOF XIII tier.

An asset is **rejected** if any of these are true:
- Anatomy errors: hands, extra or missing limbs, broken joints, or a face that changes between frames.
- Style drift from the style bible, or 3D/photoreal rendering.
- The character's identity changes between frames: outfit, colors, hair or proportions.
- The background isn't pure #00FF00 on sprites, or has shadows/gradients/text on it.
- Anything explicit or anything that reads as underage. All characters are adults (25+).
- Wrong camera angle for the asset type.

## Sprite rules
- Pure flat #00FF00 background with no floor, shadows or ground line.
- The character faces RIGHT, shown in 3/4 side view, the way Windjammers sprites face the opponent.
- The full body is visible, head to toe, with nothing cropped.
- One character per image, and the same scale for every pose in a sheet.
- Every sheet uses the approved reference sheet as its reference image.
