DISC RIOT 2084

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Short version: Disc Riot 2084 does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What the game stores

Your fighter upgrades, unlocked achievements and match stats are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage. This data never leaves your phone, and the developer cannot see it.

Deleting the app deletes this saved progress.

What the game does not do

  • No sign-in or account of any kind
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking services
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location
  • No in-app purchases
  • No internet connection needed to play: all game content is inside the app

Links to other sites

The in-game Credits screen links to the pages of the artists whose music and sounds appear in the game. Those sites have their own privacy policies; the game sends them no data about you.

Children

The game is rated for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on Disc Riot 2084's App Store or Google Play page.
